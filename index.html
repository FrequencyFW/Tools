Measurement Framework

TRACKING METRICS

Three primary variables determine lifecycle position and velocity.

Metric 01
VULNERABILITY RESERVE
Personal or professional liability held by the principal as a latent control mechanism. Higher reserve = greater compliance, lower dispensability. Accumulates through vetting, confession, SF-86 disclosures, and documented exposure.
Metric 02
EXPOSURE VECTOR
The rate at which the official becomes publicly identified with contested decisions. High exposure = high utility as shield, high liability as liability. Measured by media frequency, congressional investigation proximity, and narrative ownership.
Metric 03
LOYALTY SCORE
Not a measure of actual loyalty — a measure of perceived expendability from the principal's position. Decreases as officials accumulate independent brand, power base, or public narrative ownership.

THE TWO LAWS APPLIED

Law 1 (Truth): Truth requires zero maintenance energy. It stabilizes over time. An official who has completed the lifecycle leaves a primary source record that requires no interpretation.

Law 2 (Deception): Coordinated deception requires escalating energy to sustain. The maintenance cost of keeping an expendable official in position grows as the gap between their public role and their actual function widens. Stage 4 (Overexposure) marks the inflection point where maintenance cost exceeds utility value. Stage 7 (Sacrifice) is the energy release.
